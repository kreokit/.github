# KreoKit

**Free, open-source creator tools. No subscriptions. No watermarks. Everything runs on your own computer.**

KreoKit is a suite of desktop apps for people who make content — videos, reels, podcasts, thumbnails — built as free alternatives to the paid tools creators rely on every day.

> *Kreo* comes from the Albanian *krijo* — "create."

## The apps

| App | What it does | Free alternative to | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **[KreoKit Captions](https://github.com/kreokit/captions)** | Auto-captions for videos, word-by-word highlight, burn-in export | Paid auto-caption tools | 🧪 Alpha · [download](https://github.com/kreokit/captions/releases) |
| **KreoKit Cutout** | One-click background removal for images | Paid background removers | 📋 Planned |
| **KreoKit Clips** | Turn long videos into vertical shorts with captions | Paid clip-making tools | 📋 Planned |
| **KreoKit Voice** | Remove noise and echo from recordings | Paid noise-cancellation tools | 📋 Planned |
| **KreoKit Rec** | Screen + camera recording with a shareable file | Paid screen recorders | 📋 Planned |
| **KreoKit Design** | Thumbnails and social graphics | Paid design tools | 📋 Planned |

## Principles

- **Free forever.** Dual-licensed MIT / Apache 2.0.
- **Local first.** AI models run on your machine. Your files never leave your computer, and there are no accounts.
- **Cross-platform.** Windows, macOS and Linux.
- **English first, worldwide.** Built for creators everywhere, with 90+ languages supported through Whisper.

## How it's built

Rust + [Tauri](https://tauri.app) desktop apps with React/TypeScript interfaces, local AI models (whisper.cpp, ONNX), and FFmpeg. Developed with heavy help from Claude (Anthropic).

All apps are implemented from public specifications, open-source libraries and observed behaviour only. No proprietary code is used. KreoKit is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any company whose products are mentioned as alternatives.
